## Exact Identity And Scope

Each source goes through the accepted Friendship row Verify once, thereby
replaying the comparison, complete queue, every native request/frame/nonce and
facts. Only the row's already hash-bound native receipt metadata is read afterward;
there is no redundant second native deep verification within that source build.

The corpus derives the exact `friendships_5` or `friendships_10` identity from the
native receipt and checks the verified transition and original 19-criterion,
21-point authority. The versioned rules remain count_points_gte_1975 with targets
5 and 10 over npcs.friendships, not an invented eight-heart/2000-point threshold.
Native receipts already enforce the full pinned native population/range contract.

Rows retain original save/player/run/trajectory/split/state/candidate identities,
candidate-denominator hash and count, full queue-item/primitive/nonce arrays,
observed counts and original pair/uncompared scope. Every movement nonce and item
is included in deduplication, not just the terminal social action. Repeated native
requests/actions, copied decisions/trajectories, borrowed thresholds or altered
original partitions reject. Distinct real decisions may share one original split.

Five-person evidence cannot credit ten people. Each criterion computes its own
comparison and native partitions. Complete-order=false and the original 19/15
uncompared candidates remain retained context, not negative labels. Even a future
split-complete corpus does not certify prior friendship/story acquisition, the
whole root or formal product training.
