single blocked attempt. They retain the original queue/item IDs, order, compiled
source, fresh step sources, actual attempts/pending counts and block reasons;
no failed receipt is rewritten as successful. Every accepted successful step
must have its actual after artifact, one corresponding new crop and exactly
one decrement from the bound player slot. A blocked suffix must change no
resource or persistent progress. Unknown effects or zero verified success do
not authorize consumption. They retain their original artifacts for recovery.

metadata change, invented seed echo, extra crop, other-owner consumption, or

are physically hashed and included in the proof, together with the exact
accepted marker-ledger hash. Step paths must stay inside the receipt directory,

expected SHA and independently rebuilt native content, and never overwrites
