## Public Contract

```text
build-marriage-teacher-corpus --request ABS --output NEW_JSON
verify-marriage-teacher-corpus --corpus ABS --output NEW_JSON
```

`marriage_teacher_corpus_request.v1` contains `corpus_id`, `knowledge_path`,
`sources`, and `formal_product_training_authorized=false`. Each source contains
`source_id`, `native_receipt_path` and **every** `teacher_row_paths` from that
complete joint source. Row input order does not matter; preparation indices do.
Existing output paths reject before source replay or writes.

The authority must retain all 19 distinct criteria, 21 points and target 21.
The exact joint criterion remains one point for `player.married_or_roommate`
AND `player.farmhouse_upgrade_level>=2`, not engagement, a gift, or an OR rule.
Every preparation's authority hash must match the corpus authority.

The complete original native joint source is verified once per group. The row
builder then reuses that verified object **inside the same call** and compares
each complete original row exactly, including its provider, source/index/hash,
candidate denominator, explicit pairs and native queue. Public single-row Verify
still independently replays its source. No cross-call path-only cache is added.
Missing or foreign Teacher claims reject explicitly before source use.

The corpus retains original row hashes, preparation identities, trajectory and
candidate IDs, full denominator counts/hashes, actual queue items/primitive IDs/
nonces, uncompared IDs and complete-order flags. The entire native `queue_chain`
and joint criterion transition are retained separately. Mechanical controls never
become extra preference labels. Duplicate paths, renamed receipt copies, reused
decision/trajectory/queue/action/nonce identities and omitted preparations reject.

## Outcome And Partition Ownership

One complete joint source contributes **one native outcome**, even when it has
multiple preparation decisions. Its origin split key and partition come from the
original decision context. All preparation rows in that group must agree; the
later wedding date is not a second split or an independent evaluation sample.
Both comparison and native coverage bind this same original group partition.

The native criterion transition can occur in the final sleep's wedding setup;
the wedding event and delayed native home return still have to complete through
the original joint source verifier. Final snapshot hash need not equal the last
queue's earlier capture hash. The new corpus does not reinterpret native movement,
current-tool unknowns, ordinary menu recovery or earlier completion guards.

All original row rewards/returns remain unchanged. Linking a later joint outcome
does not backfill an immediate gift reward or invent complete long-horizon returns.
All three policy partitions are required for split completeness, but even that
would not prove prior acquisition, a complete root method, or formal admission.
