| risk id | risk | impact | mitigation | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RISK-012 | `machine_infrastructure.v1` now supplies complete current fleet occupancy, work-in-progress/due-today evidence, bounded immediate input alternatives, known machine recipes/current-inventory craftability, location split, route-hop lower bounds, projected Cellar capacity, and exact purchase liquidity use. Native personal machine crafting is statically wired, but it deliberately does not yet claim live-menu evidence, long-horizon input demand, full-cycle throughput, walking/round-trip time, machine relocation value, Cask aging value, chest/Workbench supply, or competing investment returns. | A ranker could still overvalue the 100,000g level-3 purchase when current inventory probes/materials are mistaken for durable supply, or when potential floor area is mistaken for placed productive machines. Incomplete player-chest coverage could also hide materials or generate inefficient storage routes. | Keep direct Grandpa score delta and fixed Cellar rank bonus at zero. Preserve evidence-status/semantics fields, fail-visible unclassified recipes, and compiler rebinding. After isolated crafting smoke, implement generic machine placement, then the scheduled chest layout/placement/native-transfer chain, Cask-specific valuation, plan-horizon supply, and comparable investments on one horizon. | narrowed_open |
| RISK-011 | Farmhouse purchases are irreversible and the new native Carpenter state machine has not yet been exercised in a disposable in-game save. | Dialogue timing could fail after native payment, or postcondition verification could report a false block while construction has already started. | Candidate, compiler, and runtime independently rebind endpoint, Robin presence, competing construction, house level, countdown, exact money/material tuple, and stand/path. Runtime uses only native map-action/dialogue callbacks and verifies money, material, unchanged immediate level, and countdown 3. | RISK-007 | Runtime fishing replaced `Game1.input` with a custom `ControlledInputState`, but SMAPI 4.5.2 casts it to internal `SInputState` every update. | The game froze on the first cast frame, logs grew unboundedly, the executor request never completed, and no valid fishing training sample was produced. | Global/OS input replacement was removed. Runtime now reflects only the existing SMAPI `SInputState.OverrideButton(SButton.MouseLeft, ...)` entry point during `UpdateTicking`, preserving native charge, cast, hook, BobberBar physics, and cleanup. Source guards plus hidden ordinary and Lava Eel smokes passed with zero SMAPI errors. | closed |
| RISK-008 | High-difficulty fish are not mechanically guaranteed at low character fishing level even with optimal input and legal advanced equipment. | Treating every cast as certain could bias planning and training rewards; treating executor losses as crashes would also be wrong. | Preserve observed success/failure labels and transparent fishing level, rod, bait, tackle, fish difficulty, and outcome distribution. The Lava Eel smoke proves a successful native BobberBar path, but planner utility must retain the remaining character/equipment-dependent risk. | narrowed_open |

| RISK-003 | Crop planting is only verified as a narrow single-tile executor slice, not as broad planting policy. | If a future planner exposes layout/crop-choice planting before candidate generation, seed acquisition, exceptions, and next-day outcomes are covered, the model could choose poor or impossible planting plans. | `farm.crop_catalog`, `player.seed_inventory`, and `current_location.planting_context` are transparent. `executor.plant_seed` now requires exact tile/seed `hard_rule_allows_planting=true` and has one isolated runtime smoke.
