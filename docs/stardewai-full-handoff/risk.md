# Risks

| risk id | risk | impact | mitigation | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RISK-007 | Runtime fishing replaced `Game1.input` with a custom `ControlledInputState`, but SMAPI 4.5.2 casts it to internal `SInputState` every update. | The game froze on the first cast frame, logs grew unboundedly, the executor request never completed, and no valid fishing training sample was produced. | Global/OS input replacement was removed. Runtime now reflects only the existing SMAPI `SInputState.OverrideButton(SButton.MouseLeft, ...)` entry point during `UpdateTicking`, preserving native charge, cast, hook, BobberBar physics, and cleanup. Source guards plus hidden ordinary and Lava Eel smokes passed with zero SMAPI errors. | closed |
| RISK-008 | High-difficulty fish are not mechanically guaranteed at low character fishing level even with optimal input and legal advanced equipment. | Treating every cast as certain could bias planning and training rewards; treating executor losses as crashes would also be wrong. | Preserve observed success/failure labels and transparent fishing level, rod, bait, tackle, fish difficulty, and outcome distribution. The Lava Eel smoke proves a successful native BobberBar path, but planner utility must retain the remaining character/equipment-dependent risk. | narrowed_open |
| RISK-001 | The new after-snapshot freshness wait was not exercised against a live E: SMAPI session in the first pass. | A live bridge/runtime timing edge could still produce `after_snapshot_fresh=false`, blocking trusted model training data. | Completed hidden/silent live runtime validation with applied and blocked movement episodes; both final artifacts have `after_snapshot_fresh=true` and `state_hash_changed=true`. | closed |
| RISK-002 | Historical applied movement artifact has equal before/after hashes even though executor changed `player.tile`. | Existing historical episode artifacts should not be used as final training examples for movement outcome learning. | Treat prior equal-hash movement artifacts as diagnostic only; regenerated final applied and blocked artifacts under `artifacts\hidden-training-final-*`. | closed |
| RISK-003 | Crop planting is only verified as a narrow single-tile executor slice, not as broad planting policy. | If a future planner exposes layout/crop-choice planting before candidate generation, seed acquisition, exceptions, and next-day outcomes are covered, the model could choose poor or impossible planting plans. | `farm.crop_catalog`, `player.seed_inventory`, and `current_location.planting_context` are transparent. `executor.plant_seed` now requires exact tile/seed `hard_rule_allows_planting=true` and has one isolated runtime smoke. Keep broad planting candidates/policy blocked until layout choice, buying missing seeds, fertilizer, exception maps, harvest/growth outcomes, and training output recording are covered. | narrowed_open |
| RISK-004 | Broad behavior/policy training is not meaningful while hard-rule domains are incomplete. | Model may learn from incomplete or invalid candidate spaces and overfit to smoke/calibration artifacts. | Training is paused. Resume only after `hard-rule-transparency-audit.md` blocked domains have transparent fields, Wiki confirmation, local decompile evidence, compiler gates, and tests. | open |
| RISK-005 | Shop opening and one safe money-currency purchase were runtime-verified, but broad shop behavior is not complete. | A future planner could still choose a shop/action branch outside the verified slice, or assume all shops/items are safe because Blacksmith copper ore passed. | Keep route/shop candidates gated by `locations.route_gate_context`, owner/service time, and `current_location.shop_action_tiles`. The verified slice is isolated route-to-shop plus `executor.buy_shop_item` for entries with `executor_purchase_enabled=true`; do not generalize to unsupported branches, selling, upgrades, or special shops. | narrowed_open |
| RISK-006 | Purchase `actionWhenPurchased` and UI held-item behavior can mutate state beyond simple inventory add. | A purchase executor could lose items, start tool upgrades, consume special currency, learn recipes, open new menus, or leave items in `ShopMenu.heldItem` if modeled too simply. | `menus.shop_stock` exposes affordability/trade/inventory/side-effect risk fields, and the runtime purchase executor only accepts safe whitelisted stock entries. Blacksmith `(O)378` single-item purchase is verified; callback-heavy, trade-currency, limited/synced stock, upgrades, buyback, and selling remain blocked until separately modeled and smoked. | narrowed_open |
