## Restart And Accounting

- An admitted batch may make its first original invocation once after restart.
- A native claim with retained complete evidence can finish verification/marker
  without another native invocation; unknown claims are not cleared or retried.
- Lost marker reply uses original journal/lookup before full final Get; known bad
  replies are not overwritten or accepted through fallback.
- Accepted-head restart reconstructs native outcome before checking accepted prefix
  counts/tiles/completion; latest accepted JSON is not authority by itself.
- Maintenance continues original owned route on the same sequence. Wrong seed/route
  APIs cannot consume pending maintenance.

Partial watering is not an entire crop day/cycle or Teacher preference label.
Shared resource owners remain subject to the original complete ledger/resource
projection. Formal training remains false.
